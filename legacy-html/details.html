      Health & safety

      What happens on board to keep you safe, and what to bring with you.

      Doctors and nurses are on call 24 hours a day. Basic consultations and first aid are available; charges apply for treatment and medicine.

      Every guest attends a short muster drill before departure. Life jackets in adult, child and infant sizes are stored in every cabin and at each muster station.

      Restaurants follow international food-safety standards, all food served is halal, and public areas are cleaned throughout the day.

      Bring a passport valid for at least six months, or a Saudi national ID for domestic-only sailings. Check visa needs for Egypt and Jordan before you book.

      Infants must be at least 6 months old on the departure date. Cots are free on request, and each infant must travel with an adult in the same cabin.

      No online payment. Our team contacts you to arrange payment.
